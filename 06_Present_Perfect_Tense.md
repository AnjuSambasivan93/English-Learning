# Lesson 6: Present Perfect Tense

The present perfect tense describes **past actions or situations connected to the present**.

### 1. Basic Structure

**Subject + Have/Has + Past Participle (V3)**

| Subject | Helping Verb | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I/You/We/They | Have | I have finished. |
| He/She/It | Has | She has finished. |

### 2. Verb Forms (V1, V2, V3)

| V1 (Base) | V2 (Past) | V3 (Past Participle) |
|---|---|---|
| Go | Went | Gone |
| Eat | Ate | Eaten |
| Drink | Drank | Drunk |
| See | Saw | Seen |
| Write | Wrote | Written |
| Take | Took | Taken |
| Buy | Bought | Bought |
| Work | Worked | Worked |
| Do | Did | Done |
| Finish | Finished | Finished |

**Rule:** Present Perfect = Have/Has + V3

- Incorrect: I have ate.
- Correct: I have eaten.

### 3. When to Use Present Perfect

**A. Life Experiences (No Specific Past Time)**

- I have visited Auckland.
- She has travelled to India.
- Have you ever visited Australia?

**B. Past Actions With Present Results**

- I have lost my keys. (I cannot find them now.)
- She has broken her phone. (It is damaged now.)
- I have finished my work. (It is complete now.)

**C. Actions That Started in the Past and Continue Now**

- I have lived in Christchurch since 2022.
- She has worked here for three years.
- We have known each other for five years.

### 4. Since vs For

| Since (Starting Point) | For (Duration) |
|---|---|
| Since 2022 | For four years |
| Since Monday | For three days |
| Since January | For two months |
| Since 9 AM | For five hours |

Examples:
- I have lived here since 2022.
- I have lived here for four years.

### 5. Negative Sentences

**Structure: Subject + Haven't/Hasn't + V3**

- I/You/We/They → Haven't
- He/She/It → Hasn't

Examples:
- I haven't finished my work.
- She hasn't eaten breakfast.
- They haven't arrived yet.

### 6. Questions

**Structure: Have/Has + Subject + V3?**

Examples:
- Have you finished your work?
- Has she eaten breakfast?
- Have they arrived?

Short answers:
- Yes, I have.
- No, I haven't.
- Yes, she has.
- No, she hasn't.

### 7. Ever, Never, Just, Already, Yet

| Word | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Ever | At any time | Have you ever visited India? |
| Never | Not at any time | I have never visited Japan. |
| Just | Very recently | I have just finished work. |
| Already | Before now | She has already eaten. |
| Yet | Until now | Have you finished yet? |

### 8. Past Simple vs Present Perfect

| Past Simple | Present Perfect |
|---|---|
| I ate breakfast at 8 AM. | I have eaten breakfast. |
| I visited India last year. | I have visited India. |
| She finished work yesterday. | She has finished work. |
| I lived in Auckland in 2020. | I have lived in Christchurch since 2022. |

**Past Simple:** Completed past action, often with a specific past time.

**Present Perfect:** Past action or situation connected to the present.

- Incorrect: I have visited India last year.
- Correct: I visited India last year.

### Key Points

- Positive → I have eaten.
- Negative → I haven't eaten.
- Question → Have you eaten?
- Have/Has + V3.
- Since → Starting point.
- For → Duration.
- Past Simple → Finished past event.
- Present Perfect → Connection between past and present.
