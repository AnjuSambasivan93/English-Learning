# Lesson 4: Sentence Structure and Word Order

English sentences normally follow a specific word order.

### 1. Basic Sentence Structure

**Subject + Verb + Object + Place + Time**

Example: I study English at home every evening.

| Part | Example |
|---|---|
| Subject | I |
| Verb | study |
| Object | English |
| Place | at home |
| Time | every evening |

Place usually comes before time, but other word orders are possible.

### 2. Positive Sentences

**Structure: Subject + Verb + Object**

Examples:
- I like coffee.
- She works in Christchurch.
- They play cricket.

### 3. Negative Sentences

**Structure: Subject + Don't/Doesn't + Base Verb**

- I/You/We/They → Don't
- He/She/It → Doesn't

Examples:
- I don't drink coffee.
- She doesn't work on Sundays.
- They don't play cricket.

**Rule:** After doesn't, always use the base verb.

- Incorrect: She doesn't likes coffee.
- Correct: She doesn't like coffee.

With am/is/are, add **not** directly:
- I am not tired.
- She is not ready.
- They are not busy.

### 4. Questions

**Structure: Do/Does + Subject + Base Verb?**

Examples:
- Do you work here?
- Does she like coffee?
- Do they speak English?

With am/is/are, change the word order:
- She is happy. → Is she happy?
- They are ready. → Are they ready?

### 5. WH Questions

| Word | Purpose | Example |
|---|---|---|
| What | Thing/Action | What do you do? |
| Where | Place | Where do you live? |
| When | Time | When do you work? |
| Why | Reason | Why are you late? |
| Who | Person | Who is your manager? |
| How | Manner | How do you travel? |
| Which | Choice | Which car do you prefer? |

**Structure: WH Word + Do/Does + Subject + Base Verb?**

Examples:
- Where do you work?
- What does she study?
- When do they arrive?

With am/is/are:
- Where are you?
- Why is he upset?

**Exception:** When who is the subject, we can ask "Who works here?" without do/does.

### 6. Position of Adverbs

Common frequency adverbs: Always, Usually, Often, Sometimes, Never.

**Rule 1:** Usually before the main verb.

- I always drink coffee.
- She usually walks to work.
- They sometimes eat outside.

**Rule 2:** Usually after am/is/are.

- He is always busy.
- They are usually happy.
- I am never late.

### 7. Common Mistakes

| Incorrect | Correct |
|---|---|
| I every day study English. | I study English every day. |
| Where you work? | Where do you work? |
| Why she is late? | Why is she late? |
| He doesn't likes tea. | He doesn't like tea. |
| She always is busy. | She is always busy. |

### Key Points

- Basic order: Subject + Verb + Object + Place + Time.
- Negative: Don't/Doesn't + Base Verb.
- Questions: Do/Does + Subject + Base Verb.
- WH Questions: WH Word + Do/Does + Subject + Base Verb.
- Frequency adverbs usually come before main verbs but after am/is/are.
