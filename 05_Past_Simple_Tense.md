# Lesson 5: Past Simple Tense

The past simple tense is used to describe **actions completed in the past**.

### 1. Basic Structure

| Type | Structure | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Positive | Subject + Past Verb | I worked yesterday. |
| Negative | Subject + Didn't + Base Verb | I didn't work yesterday. |
| Question | Did + Subject + Base Verb? | Did you work yesterday? |

### 2. Regular Verbs

Regular verbs usually form the past tense by adding **-ed**.

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| Most verbs → +ed | Work → Worked |
| Ends in e → +d | Live → Lived |
| Consonant + y → ied | Study → Studied |
| Some short verbs → Double consonant + ed | Stop → Stopped |

Examples:
- I worked yesterday.
- She played football.
- They visited Auckland.

### 3. Irregular Verbs

Irregular verbs do not follow the -ed rule.

| Present | Past |
|---|---|
| Go | Went |
| Eat | Ate |
| Drink | Drank |
| Buy | Bought |
| See | Saw |
| Come | Came |
| Take | Took |
| Give | Gave |
| Have | Had |
| Do | Did |
| Make | Made |
| Get | Got |
| Write | Wrote |
| Speak | Spoke |

Examples:
- I went to work yesterday.
- She bought a car.
- We ate dinner.

### 4. Negative Sentences

**Structure: Subject + Didn't + Base Verb**

Examples:
- I didn't go to work.
- She didn't eat breakfast.
- They didn't watch the movie.

**Rule:** After didn't, always use the base verb.

- Incorrect: I didn't went.
- Correct: I didn't go.

### 5. Questions

**Structure: Did + Subject + Base Verb?**

Examples:
- Did you go to work?
- Did she call you?
- Did they finish the project?

Short answers:
- Yes, I did.
- No, I didn't.

**WH Questions: WH Word + Did + Subject + Base Verb?**

Examples:
- Where did you go?
- What did you eat?
- When did she arrive?
- Why did he leave?

Exception: When **who** is the subject, we can ask "Who called you?" without did.

### 6. Was and Were

Past forms of am, is and are.

| Subject | Past Form |
|---|---|
| I/He/She/It | Was |
| You/We/They | Were |

Examples:
- I was tired yesterday.
- She was at home.
- They were happy.

Negative:
- I wasn't tired.
- They weren't at home.

Questions:
- Was she happy?
- Were you busy?

**Rule:** Do not use did with was/were in ordinary past simple questions.

### 7. Past Time Expressions

Common expressions:
- Yesterday
- Last night/week/month/year
- Two days ago
- In 2020

Examples:
- I finished my work two hours ago.
- She visited India last year.
- We moved to Christchurch in 2022.

### Key Points

- Positive → I went.
- Negative → I didn't go.
- Question → Did you go?
- Was/Were → I was tired. They were busy.
- After did/didn't → Always use the base verb.
- Past simple → Completed actions in the past.
