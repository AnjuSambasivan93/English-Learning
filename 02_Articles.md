# Lesson 2: Articles (A, An, The)

Articles are words used before nouns.

### 1. A - General (Consonant Sound)

Use **a** before a singular countable noun beginning with a consonant sound.

Examples:
- I bought a car.
- She reads a book.
- He is a doctor.
- A university (starts with a "you" sound).

### 2. An - General (Vowel Sound)

Use **an** before a singular countable noun beginning with a vowel sound.

Examples:
- I ate an apple.
- She sent an email.
- He bought an orange.
- An hour (silent "h").

**Note:** Use a/an based on sound, not spelling.

### 3. The - Specific or Known

Use **the** when referring to something specific or already known.

Examples:
- I bought a car. The car is red.
- Please close the door.
- Can you pass me the salt?
- The sun is shining.

**A car** = Any car (not specific).

**The car** = A particular car (specific).

### 4. No Article (Zero Article)

Do not use an article with plural countable nouns or uncountable nouns when speaking generally.

Examples:
- I like coffee.
- She loves music.
- Dogs are friendly.
- I drink milk.
- We buy books.

### 5. Quick Comparison

| Article | Usage | Example |
|---|---|---|
| A | General, consonant sound | A car |
| An | General, vowel sound | An apple |
| The | Specific or known | The car |
| No article | General plural or uncountable noun | I like music |

### 6. Important Differences

- I like coffee. (Coffee in general)
- I like the coffee you made. (Specific coffee)
- I like dogs. (Dogs in general)
- I like the dogs in your house. (Specific dogs)

**Remember:** A/An = General, The = Specific, No article = General plural or uncountable nouns.
