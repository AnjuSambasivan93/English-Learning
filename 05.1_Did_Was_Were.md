# Lesson 5.1: Did vs Was/Were

### 1. Did - Past Actions

Use **did** to ask about actions completed in the past.

**Structure:** Did + Subject + Base Verb?

Examples:

- Did you work yesterday?
- Did she eat breakfast?
- Did they finish the project?

**Rule:** After did, use the base verb.

- Incorrect: Did you went?
- Correct: Did you go?

### 2. Was/Were — Past State or Condition

Use **was/were** to ask about past feelings, conditions, situations or locations.

| Subject        | Verb |
| -------------- | ---- |
| I, He, She, It | Was  |
| You, We, They  | Were |

Examples:

- Was she happy?
- Were you busy?
- Was he sick?
- Were they at home?

### 3. Did vs Was/Were

| Did (Action)          | Was/Were (State)   |
| --------------------- | ------------------ |
| Did you work?         | Were you busy?     |
| Did she go to school? | Was she at school? |
| Did he eat?           | Was he hungry?     |
| Did they play?        | Were they happy?   |

### 4. Where Did vs Where Was/Were

- Where did you go? → I went to work. (Action)
- Where were you? → I was at work. (Location)

### Key Points

- **Did + Base Verb** → Past action.
- **Was/Were** → Past state, feeling, condition or location.
- Do not use did with was/were in simple past questions.
- Incorrect: Did you were busy?
- Correct: Were you busy?
