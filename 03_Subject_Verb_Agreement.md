# Lesson 3: Subject-Verb Agreement

Subject-verb agreement means **the verb must match the subject**.

### 1. Basic Rule (Present Simple)

- **I, You, We, They** → Base verb
- **He, She, It** → Verb + s/es

| Subject | Verb | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I/You/We/They | work | They work here. |
| He/She/It | works | She works here. |

### 2. Adding S, ES, IES

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| Most verbs → +s | Work → Works |
| Ends in ch, sh, ss, x, z → +es | Watch → Watches |
| Consonant + y → ies | Study → Studies |
| Vowel + y → +s | Play → Plays |
| Irregular forms | Go → Goes, Do → Does, Have → Has |

### 3. Am, Is, Are

| Subject | Verb | Example |
|---|---|---|
| I | am | I am happy. |
| He/She/It | is | She is busy. |
| You/We/They | are | They are ready. |

### 4. Has and Have

- **I/You/We/They** → Have
- **He/She/It** → Has

Examples:
- I have a car.
- She has a laptop.
- My friends have cars.

### 5. Do and Does

Used for questions and negative sentences in the present simple.

- **I/You/We/They** → Do / Don't
- **He/She/It** → Does / Doesn't

Examples:
- Do you work here?
- Does she work here?
- I don't like coffee.
- He doesn't like tea.

**Important:** After does/doesn't, use the base verb.

- Incorrect: Does she works here?
- Correct: Does she work here?

### 6. Singular and Plural Subjects

- **Singular subject** → Singular verb
- **Plural subject** → Plural verb

Examples:
- The student studies English.
- The students study English.
- My friend lives in Christchurch.
- My friends live in Christchurch.

### Key Points

- He/She/It → Works, Eats, Studies
- I/You/We/They → Work, Eat, Study
- After does/doesn't → Base verb
- I am / He is / They are
- He has / They have
